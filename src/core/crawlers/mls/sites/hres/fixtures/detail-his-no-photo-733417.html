A unique opportunity to purchase an extensively improved parcel in Mark Twain Estates with substantial water infrastructure, mature gardens, farm improvements, off-grid systems, and a custom tiny house. This property has been actively lived on, farmed, landscaped and improved over a number of years. The result is a mature and functional property with cleared areas, established pathways, productive growing spaces, multiple structures and significant site work already completed. A major advantage is the extra large 1 1/2 in. waterline running onto the property along with a professinally installed backflow prevention system. The parcel includes two previously cleared and leveled house-pad areas, which enjoy dramatic hill and ocean views. The property is home to a small working mushroom, herb and botanical farm with exceptionally fertile and organically enriched soil built up with macadamia husk, coffee parchment, local hardwood sawdust and spent mushroom farm substrate creating rich, productive, moisture holding soil throughout the gardens. A diverse collection of established edible, medicinal, ornamental plants enhance the property. An unpermitted tiny house on a trailer with finished interior, windows , flooring, custom woodwork and trim will be conveyed as-is. Included also is a 16'X16 farm shelter currently used as a kitchen and living area, with an existing 2,000 watt inverter connected to a 600-watt six panel solar system with a 300 ah LiFe P04 battery and approx. 15 cu. ft. refrigerator and a propane hot water heater providing useful off-grid infrastructure from day one.
