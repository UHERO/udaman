Thank you for visiting HawaiiRealEstateSearch.com 's search page for ALL of Hawaii and allowing us to be a part of your search. Our Hawaii MLS Search feature is filled with advanced technological advances to provide users with the most updated information in an easy-to-search platform. Our data is updated every 15 minutes directly from the all of the MLS's serving Hawaii, so our site visitors can rest assured the latest listings are just a click away. 

Our innovative search platform allows our users to search using the same level of granular detail as Realtors do. 

Quick Search - The 'Location' field on the upper left hand is a 'catch all' which searches all relevant fields. For example, type in 'Wailea' (which is part of a district) or 'Moana Estates' (which is a subdivision) and all relevant properties are shown in the results. Searching all Hawaii MLS's made easy!

Want to get a more specific sub-set? Then search by any combination of the following factors:

Vacation Rental: With each County having different rules and definitions that govern whether a property is allowed to do vacation rentals, looking for an investment property throughout the State that allows such usage can be quite daunting. We have manually curated results in a manner that cover all of Hawaii. As a caveat, for Oahu, these are properties zoned X-5 and X-6, but there may be underlying property-specific rules that may further restrict (such as Allure requiring 30 day rentals).

Virtual Tours: In the post-pandemic world, doing things virtually have become a new norm and one which savvy users are expecting. As such, we have created THE largest searchable database of properties with virtual tours. At any given time, we have anywhere between 500 to 1,000 properties with 3-D immersive virtual tours. View properties from the comfort and safety of your home.

Price Range - Our intuitive 'Price Range' parameters are set in logical boundaries, meant to best encapsulate how people search for real estate, without adding too many unnecessary steps.

City - Whether defined as a district or city, our award-winning, innovative database searches all.

Property Type - The breakdown is easy to follow and gives you exactly the type of property you are searching for. Residential (single-family home), Condominium, and Vacant Land are the most commonly searched.

Search by Condo - This is a particular useful field for those who have narrowed down their search and know the complex in which they'd like to find their property? Looking for a Honua Kai condo for sale or a penthouse at Park Lane? Simply type in the condo name and voila.

Subdivision - Same principle as with the condo search, type in the specifics of your interest subdivision (i.e. Diamond Head, Wailea Kai) and the results are all inclusive of that term.

Bedrooms and Bathrooms - Switch the parameters between your minimum and maximum requirements.

MLS Number - Occasionally, buyers know the exact MLS number for the property they are searching for. Depending on the MLS, it is a six-digit number starting with the number '3' (Maui), a multi-number ID starting with the year (Oahu) or a longer six digit MLS number starting with a '6' (Kauai and Big Island)

Year Built - Looking for a newer build home or condo? Toggle the Minimum and Maximum year to look at those that meet your parameters.

Waterfront - We've have merged all MLS's to simplify 'waterfront' in a more universal method, look throughout the entire State for Hawaii beachfront properties for sale.

Days on Market - For those actively searching the Maui real estate market, this can be a VERY useful tool. Only looking for this week's listings of Kihei Homes for sale? Make the right selection and the newest listings are yours to see.
